E4 CONFIGURATION DATA

These files support Objective E4:
- treatment_guideline_config.csv
- hospital_treatment_availability.csv
- treatment_resource_config.csv

IMPORTANT:
All values are PROJECT/SYNTHETIC CONFIGURATION VALUES.
They are not real clinical guideline evidence, hospital inventory,
procurement data, or treatment prices.

PROJECT_SUPPORTED means the treatment is supported by the project's
disease/severity treatment mapping. It does not mean national guideline
approval.

Availability values are synthetic and are intended for prototype testing.

LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH are relative resource/cost tiers, not rupee prices.
